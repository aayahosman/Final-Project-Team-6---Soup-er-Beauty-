# CS 315 Final Project: Team 6 - Soup-er Beauty

## Reinforcing and Challenging Beauty Standards: Audience Reception to Beauty in Korean Dramas

### Project Overview
This project examines how beauty standards are portrayed in South Korean television dramas (K-dramas) and how international audiences perceive, discuss, and respond to them. Using threads, comments, and reviews collected from Reddit and MyDramaList, we apply textual and sentiment analysis to explore these discussions surrounding physical beauty, harsh  standards, societal expecations, and more themes. 

In order to conduct this analysis, we focus on five specific K-dramas:
- *Mask Girl* (2023)
- *True Beauty* (2020)
- *My ID Is Gangnam Beauty* (2018)
- *Birth of a Beauty* (2014)
- *Boys Over Flowers* (2009)

### Research Questions
We are interested in:
1. What beauty standards are discussed most frequently in audience discussions and reviews of K-dramas?
2. How do online audiences perceive and respond to beauty standards portrayed in K-dramas?
3. How do discussions of beauty standards differ across K-dramas released during different time periods?
